# Phase 5 — Forms (decoupled steps)

> Goal (per `docs/05_ROADMAP.md`): fill, create, and manage interactive
> PDF forms (AcroForm). XFA gets read-only degraded support only.

## Acceptance demo (from roadmap)

- Fill `tests/fixtures/acceptance/p5-irs-w9.pdf` (real IRS W-9). Save. Open in Acrobat: all fields show the values; the form is still interactive.
- Create a new form with 8 fields of mixed types. Export filled data → re-import to a blank copy → fields restored identically.

## Spec coverage

`P5-FORM-001` through `P5-FORM-010` (10 spec lines). Plus 1 infrastructure step.

---

## Track A — Form detection & filling

### P5.A1. `[~]` Detect AcroForm + Form-mode entry point
- **Spec:** P5-FORM-001
- **What:** On open, inspect the AcroForm dict; if present, surface a "Form mode" button with field count.
- **Test:** `src-tauri/tests/form_detect.rs`.
- **Depends on:** P1.B1.

### P5.A2. `[~]` Fill text fields
- **Spec:** P5-FORM-002
- **What:** Type, Tab navigation, respect max-length and multi-line flags.
- **Test:** `src-tauri/tests/form_fill_text.rs`.
- **Depends on:** P5.A1.

### P5.A3. `[~]` Fill checkbox / radio
- **Spec:** P5-FORM-003
- **Test:** `src-tauri/tests/form_fill_checkbox.rs`.
- **Depends on:** P5.A1.

### P5.A4. `[~]` Fill choice fields (combo, list)
- **Spec:** P5-FORM-004
- **Test:** `src-tauri/tests/form_fill_choice.rs`.
- **Depends on:** P5.A1.

### P5.A5. `[~]` XFA degraded support
- **Spec:** P5-FORM-005
- **What:** When the PDF is XFA-only (no AcroForm fallback), display "XFA editing not supported" and offer "Convert XFA to flat content (read-only)."
- **Test:** `src-tauri/tests/form_xfa_degraded.rs`.
- **Depends on:** P5.A1.
